About

An applied mathematician building AI systems

Mathematical modeling is not a background detail in my work — it is the method. AI engineering is how I make it operational.

Academic journey

I study Applied Mathematics and Modeling at the Higher National Engineering School of Tunis (ENSIT), where statistics, probability, optimization and numerical methods form the backbone of how I think about problems.

Applied exposure

Across four internships I have worked on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and generative AI — building recommenders, video-analysis pipelines and cloud RAG architectures rather than notebooks alone.

Motivation

I want technology to solve problems that matter. Healthcare and finance interest me most, because in those domains a model's error has a real cost for real people.

How I work

I start from the mathematical structure of a problem — what is being estimated, what is being optimised, what the data actually supports — and then bring modern AI techniques to bear on it.

Where I'm heading

I'm looking for international AI/ML research internships, PFE (end-of-study) opportunities and research collaborations where mathematical rigour and engineering practice meet.
